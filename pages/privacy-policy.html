Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Ultimate Online Calculator ("we", "us") collects, how it's used, and the choices you have. This is a frontend-only website: the calculators themselves run in your browser and do not transmit your calculator inputs to a server.

Information we collect

We do not require account registration and do not collect personal information to let you use a calculator. If you contact us through the Contact form, we receive the name, email address, and message you choose to provide, via our third-party form provider, FormSubmit.

Local storage

Favorites, calculation history, recently used calculators, and your dark/light theme preference are stored using your browser's local storage. This data stays on your device and is not transmitted to us. You can clear it at any time using your browser settings or the in-page "clear" controls.

Cookies and advertising

We intend to use Google AdSense to display advertising on this site. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings. See our Cookie Policy for more detail.

Analytics

We may use privacy-conscious analytics tools to understand aggregate usage, such as which calculators are used most often. Analytics data is used in aggregate and is not used to identify you personally.

Third-party services

Our Contact form is processed by FormSubmit, an independent third-party service. Advertising, if enabled, is served by Google AdSense. Each service operates under its own privacy policy.

Your rights

You may request details of any personal information we hold (limited to messages sent via our Contact form) or request its deletion by emailing us. You can also clear all locally stored favorites, history, and preferences directly in your browser at any time.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent via the Contact page or to lemabarijoyful@gmail.com.
