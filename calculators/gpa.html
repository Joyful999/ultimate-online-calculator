Formula

GPA = Σ(Grade Points × Credits) ÷ Σ(Credits)

Grade points follow the standard 4.0 scale (A = 4.0, B = 3.0, and so on). Check your institution's exact scale if you need an official figure.

Worked Examples

Calculus I (A, 4 credits), History (B+, 3 credits), Chemistry (B, 4 credits), Elective (A-, 2 credits): total grade points = 45.3, total credits = 13, GPA = 45.3 ÷ 13 = 3.48.

How to Use the GPA Calculator

  1. For each course, select the letter grade earned and enter its credit hours.
  2. Use "+ Add Another Course" for more rows, or double-click a row to remove it.
  3. Press Calculate to see your GPA.
