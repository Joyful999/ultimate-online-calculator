Formula

M = P × [r(1+r)^n] ÷ [(1+r)^n − 1]

Where M is the monthly payment, P is the principal, r is the monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12), and n is the number of monthly payments.

Worked Examples

A $20,000 loan at 6.5% annual interest over 5 years (60 payments) has a monthly payment of approximately $391.32, with total interest of about $3,479 over the life of the loan.

How to Use the Loan Calculator

  1. Enter the loan amount you're borrowing.
  2. Enter the annual interest rate (APR) offered.
  3. Enter the loan term in years.
  4. Press Calculate to see your estimated monthly payment and total interest.
