Formula

Sale Price = Original Price × (1 − Discount% ÷ 100)

Worked Examples

A $120 item at 25% off: savings = $30, sale price = $90.

How to Use the Discount Calculator

  1. Enter the original price.
  2. Enter the discount percentage.
  3. Press Calculate to see the amount saved and the final sale price.
