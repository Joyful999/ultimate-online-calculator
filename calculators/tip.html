Formula

Per Person = (Bill + Bill × Tip%) ÷ Number of People

Worked Examples

A $64.50 bill with an 18% tip, split between 3 people: tip = $11.61, total = $76.11, per person = $25.37.

How to Use the Tip Calculator

  1. Enter the bill amount.
  2. Enter the tip percentage you want to leave.
  3. Enter how many people are splitting the bill.
  4. Press Calculate to see the total and per-person share.
