Formula

A = P × (1 + r/n)^(n×t)

A is the final amount, P is the principal, r is the annual rate as a decimal, n is the number of compounds per year, and t is time in years.

Worked Examples

$5,000 invested at 6% for 10 years, compounded monthly, grows to approximately $9,096.98 — see our full compound interest guide for the step-by-step breakdown.

How to Use the Compound Interest Calculator

  1. Enter your starting amount.
  2. Enter the annual interest rate and how often it compounds.
  3. Enter the number of years you'll leave it invested.
  4. Optionally add a monthly contribution to see how regular saving accelerates growth.
