Formula

Needed = (Target − Current × (1 − Weight)) ÷ Weight

Where Weight is the final exam's weight as a decimal (e.g. 30% = 0.3).

Worked Examples

Current grade 78%, target 85%, final worth 30%: needed = (85 − 78×0.7) ÷ 0.3 = (85 − 54.6) ÷ 0.3 = 101.3% — meaning the target isn't achievable even with a perfect score, so aiming slightly lower would be realistic.

How to Use the Grade Calculator

  1. Enter your current grade so far.
  2. Enter the overall grade you're aiming for.
  3. Enter how much the final exam is worth as a percentage of the total grade.
  4. Press Calculate to see the score you need.
