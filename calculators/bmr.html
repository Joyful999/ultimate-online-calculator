Formula

This calculator uses the widely used Mifflin-St Jeor equation:

Male: BMR = 10×kg + 6.25×cm − 5×age + 5

Female: BMR = 10×kg + 6.25×cm − 5×age − 161

Worked Examples

A 30-year-old male, 175 cm, 70 kg: BMR = (10×70) + (6.25×175) − (5×30) + 5 = 700 + 1093.75 − 150 + 5 = 1,648.75 kcal/day.
