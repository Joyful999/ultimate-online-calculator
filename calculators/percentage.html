Formula

X% of a number:

Result = (Percentage ÷ 100) × Number

What percent is A of B:

Percentage = (A ÷ B) × 100

Worked Examples

25% of 200 = (25 ÷ 100) × 200 = 50.

45 is what percent of 300? (45 ÷ 300) × 100 = 15%.
