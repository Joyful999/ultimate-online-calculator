Formula

Maintenance Calories = BMR × Activity Multiplier

BMR is calculated using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, then scaled by an activity multiplier ranging from 1.2 (sedentary) to 1.9 (extremely active).

Worked Examples

A BMR of 1,649 kcal/day with moderate activity (×1.55): maintenance calories ≈ 2,556 kcal/day.

How to Use the Calorie Calculator

  2. Select the activity level that best matches your typical week.
