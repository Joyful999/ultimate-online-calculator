Formula

Every unit is converted to metres as a common reference, then converted to your target unit: Result = (Value × Source Factor) ÷ Target Factor. For example, 1 inch = 0.0254 metres, and 1 mile = 1,609.344 metres.

Worked Examples

10 metres converted to feet: 10 ÷ 0.3048 = 32.81 feet.

How to Use the Length Converter

  1. Enter the value you want to convert.
  2. Choose the unit you're converting from.
  3. Choose the unit you're converting to.
  4. Press Calculate to see the result.
