Formula

°F = °C × 9/5 + 32　·　K = °C + 273.15

Worked Examples

20°C converted to Fahrenheit: (20 × 9/5) + 32 = 68°F.

How to Use the Temperature Converter

Frequently Asked Questions

0°C equals 32°F and 273.15 K.

No — Kelvin is an absolute temperature scale starting at absolute zero, so negative Kelvin values aren't physically valid and this calculator flags them.

The two scales were defined using different historical reference points — the conversion formula accounts for both the different zero point and different degree size.
