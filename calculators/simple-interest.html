Formula

Interest = P × r × t

P is the principal, r is the annual rate as a decimal, and t is time in years.

Worked Examples

$10,000 at 5% for 3 years: Interest = 10000 × 0.05 × 3 = $1,500, for a total of $11,500.

How to Use the Simple Interest Calculator

  1. Enter the principal amount.
  2. Enter the annual interest rate.
  3. Enter the time period in years.
  4. Press Calculate to see interest earned and the total amount.

Frequently Asked Questions

Simple interest is calculated only on the original principal every period, while compound interest is calculated on the principal plus any interest already accrued — see our comparison guide for the full picture.

Enter years as a decimal — for example, 6 months is 0.5 years.

Most mortgages use compound interest amortization, not simple interest — use the Loan Calculator for mortgage-style payments instead.
