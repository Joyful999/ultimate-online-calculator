Formula

The total number of days is the absolute difference between the two dates on the calendar, which is then converted into weeks and an approximate month count.

Worked Examples

From 1 January 2026 to 14 August 2026 is 225 days, or 32 weeks and 1 day.

How to Use the Date Difference Calculator

  1. Choose a start date.
  2. Choose an end date.
  3. Press Calculate to see the total days, weeks and months between them.
