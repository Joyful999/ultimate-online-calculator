Formula

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height (m)²

Worked Examples

A person weighing 70 kg at 1.75 m tall: BMI = 70 ÷ 1.75² = 70 ÷ 3.0625 = 22.9 — within the healthy weight range.

How to Use the BMI Calculator
