Formula

Margin % = (Selling Price − Cost) ÷ Selling Price × 100

Markup % = (Selling Price − Cost) ÷ Cost × 100

Worked Examples

Cost $40, sold for $65: profit = $25, margin = 25/65 × 100 = 38.5%, markup = 25/40 × 100 = 62.5%.

How to Use the Profit Margin Calculator

  1. Enter your cost price (what the item cost you).
  2. Enter your selling price.
  3. Press Calculate to see gross profit, margin and markup.
