Formula

Average = Sum of all numbers ÷ Count of numbers

Worked Examples

For 12, 18, 25, 30, 9: sum = 94, count = 5, average = 94 ÷ 5 = 18.8.

How to Use the Average Calculator

  1. Type or paste your list of numbers, separated by commas or spaces.
  2. Press Calculate.
  3. Read the mean, median, minimum and maximum in the result panel.
