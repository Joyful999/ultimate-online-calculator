Formula

Per Person = (Bill Total + Extra Fee) ÷ Number of People

Worked Examples

A $150 bill plus a $20 delivery fee, split between 4 people: grand total = $170, per person = $42.50.

How to Use the Split Bill Calculator

  1. Enter the bill total.
  2. Enter how many people are splitting it.
  3. Optionally add an extra fee or tip to include in the split.
  4. Press Calculate to see each person's share.

Frequently Asked Questions

This calculator handles even splits. For itemized splitting, add up each person's individual items separately, then use this tool just for any shared costs like delivery fees.

Include tax in your bill total before entering it, or add it using the extra fee field.

This calculator assumes an even split — for custom proportions, calculate each person's share individually outside the tool.
