Formula

Age is found by taking the difference in years between the birth date and today, then subtracting one year if this year's birthday hasn't happened yet — followed by calculating the remaining months and days precisely.

Worked Examples

Born 14 September 2000, calculated as of 25 August 2026: age is 25 years, 11 months, 11 days, since this year's birthday hasn't occurred yet.

How to Use the Age Calculator

  1. Enter the date of birth.
  2. Optionally set a specific 'as of' date — otherwise today's date is used.
  3. Press Calculate to see the exact age, plus total days and weeks lived.
